# Demo script (about 4 minutes)

Open https://monmate.github.io/meghlaya-landslide-watch/ on a laptop, and on a
phone for Car Mode. Check the badge at the top: **Live** means real rain;
**Sample data** means the real build has not finished.

1. **The map (30 s).** "Every main road in Meghalaya, cut into 200 m pieces.
   Colour, line width and shape all show risk, so it works for colour-blind
   users too." Point at the rain timeline under the map.
2. **Play the timeline (30 s).** Press **Play**. "This runs from 24 hours ago to
   48 hours ahead. Bars are rain per hour; the strip shows the worst road risk.
   Risk follows the rain, but only where the terrain is steep."
3. **Why is this road red? (30 s).** Click a red or purple stretch. The popup
   gives the reasons: 24-hour rain in IMD's categories, slope, relief, cleared
   ground, reported slides.
4. **Replay a real storm (45 s).** Click **Replay June 2022 storm**, tick
   **Reported slides**, press **Play**. "On 17 June 2022 slides killed people in
   East Khasi Hills. Here is what this rule would have shown." Say it plainly:
   this is the rule applied to reanalysis rain, not a validated forecast.
5. **Plan a trip (45 s).** Switch back with **Show live**. Open **Plan a trip**,
   pick two towns, **Check this trip**. Compare Fastest and Safer, and read
   **Best time to leave**.
6. **Car Mode (45 s, on the phone).** **Start Car Mode**, **Turn on voice
   alerts**, **Simulate the drive**. The phone speaks before a risky stretch.
7. **Honesty (15 s).** Open **How this works**: sources, weights, the IMD
   categories, and how few dated slides exist, which is why the rules are not
   yet fitted. Mention what MSDMA and PWD records would unlock.
