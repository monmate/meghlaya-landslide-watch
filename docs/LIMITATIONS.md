# Known limits

- **Not validated.** Weights and thresholds are documented defaults, not fitted
  to landslide records. The map shows where the rules say risk is higher, not
  where a slide will happen.
- **Coarse rain.** Rain comes from weather models on a ~25 km grid, not gauges.
  Intense local downpours on the Khasi and Jaintia escarpments can be missed or
  underestimated. "Now" is partly modelled, not measured.
- **Forecasts.** Beyond about 24 hours, forecast rain for convective storms is
  uncertain; treat later hours as rough.
- **Surface model.** The DEM includes tree tops and buildings, so slope under
  forest carries error.
- **Invisible causes.** Mining, quarrying, road widening, cut-slope works and
  blocked drains cause failures this map cannot see.
- **Inventory bias.** Reported slides cluster near towns and big roads; no
  report does not mean no slide.
- **Network.** Roads outside Meghalaya, one-way rules, closures and live
  traffic are not included. Travel times use assumed speeds.
- **Reports.** The report form is a demo; nothing is sent or moderated yet.
- **Languages.** Hindi is a draft that needs native review. Khasi, Garo and
  Pnar are not yet available.
- **Devices.** Screen wake lock and Hindi voices depend on the phone and
  browser.
